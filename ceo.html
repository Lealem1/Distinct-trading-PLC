Our Story

Distinct Trading PLC has established itself as a premier import-export company in Ethiopia, dedicated to facilitating international trade with excellence and integrity. Our journey began with a vision to connect Ethiopian agricultural products with global markets while bringing essential industrial and manufacturing inputs to support Ethiopia's growing economy.

Today, we are proud to be a trusted partner for international buyers and suppliers, known for our commitment to quality, reliability, and ethical business practices. Our extensive network and deep understanding of both local and international markets enable us to deliver exceptional value to our clients.

Our Mission

To facilitate ethical and efficient international trade that benefits Ethiopian farmers, industries, and global partners.
